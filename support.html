Support

StatCE is a personal log for continuing education, licenses, and certifications. It is not an official board or government service and does not guarantee compliance with any licensing body.

For help, email  laurenuf@icloud.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Do not send license numbers or certificate images unless we specifically ask and you are comfortable sharing them.

Your records stay on your device. Deleting the app removes the local data unless you have an iCloud device backup that includes the app.
