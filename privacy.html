This policy covers the iOS app StatCE(the “App”), which helps you track continuing education, licenses, and certifications. Replace “CE Vault” and the contact email below with your real app name and email before you publish this page.

Who we are

The App is published by [Your legal name]. Contact: laurenuf@icloud.com.

Data stored on your device

Information you enter stays on your iPhone. That can include license and certification names, issuing bodies, license numbers, issue and expiration dates, continuing-education titles, providers, hours, notes, and photos or PDFs of certificates you choose to attach.

This data is stored locally with the app (SwiftData / on-device storage). We do not operate an account system, and we do not receive a copy of your records.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your license records, certificate images, or continuing-education log. The App does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

Apple’s definition of “collect” is data sent off the device in a way we can access. Because your records stay on the device, we do not treat them as collected by us.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. Those reminders are not sent to our servers.

Exports

If you export a CSV or PDF, that file is created on your device and shared only through the iOS share sheet to a destination you choose (Mail, Files, and so on).

Photos and files

If you attach a certificate photo or PDF, the App asks for access only so you can pick that file. We do not upload it.

Children

The App is a professional record-keeping tool and is not directed at children under 13.
